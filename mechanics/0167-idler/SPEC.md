# 0167-idler: Stellar Reactor Idler

A strategic sci-fi idle automation and thermal management simulation. Players engineer a planetary fusion reactor network, balancing energy output, neutron enrichment, cryogenic coolant cycles, and quantum prestige collapses to power a full Dyson Sphere.

## Core Pillars & Mechanics

- **Energy Economy & Automated Idle Yields**:
  - `energy`: Primary power stored (used to construct reactor facilities).
  - `neutrons`: Advanced subatomic particles generated by fusion cores (used for prestige upgrades and high-tier relays).
  - `darkMatter`: Transcendent prestige currency harvested during Quantum Overdrive resets (grants +50% compounding energy multiplier per unit).
- **Automated Generator Modules**:
  - `solenoid`: +2 energy / turn.
  - `injector`: +10 energy / turn, +0.5 neutrons / turn.
  - `tokamak`: +50 energy / turn, +2.5 neutrons / turn.
  - `antimatterCore`: +250 energy / turn, +10 neutrons / turn.
  - `dysonRelay`: +1,200 energy / turn, +40 neutrons / turn.
- **Thermal Equilibrium & Overclock Dynamics**:
  - `coreTemp`: (0-100%). Operating between 60-90% provides a supercritical thermodynamic bonus (+50% to +100% production).
  - Meltdown Hazard: Exceeding 100% causes catastrophic core containment breach and instant defeat.
  - Cryogenic Coolant: Restores thermal safety margin (-35% heat).
- **Prestige & Victory**:
  - `Quantum Overdrive`: Resets energy and generators when accumulating >= 150 neutrons, awarding Dark Matter shards.
  - `Stellar Ignition`: Unlocked at Dyson Relay tier with >= 3 Dark Matter and >= 50,000 Energy, triggering celestial Dyson Sphere ignition for Victory!
  - Max Turns: 120 turns guarantee.

## State Schema
- `seed`: integer
- `prngState`: integer
- `turns`: integer (0-120)
- `maxTurns`: 120
- `energy`: integer
- `neutrons`: integer
- `darkMatter`: integer
- `coreTemp`: integer (0-100)
- `overclockTurns`: integer
- `generators`: object
  - `solenoid`: integer
  - `injector`: integer
  - `tokamak`: integer
  - `antimatterCore`: integer
  - `dysonRelay`: integer
- `prestigeCount`: integer
- `victory`: boolean
- `defeat`: boolean
- `gameMessage`: string
